## 5. Process

### Home: "From brief to dismantle in five stages" (H2)
You get one project manager for the whole build. That person handles the organiser, the venue, the contractors and the logistics, so your marketing team is not chasing five suppliers in show week.
1. **Brief and floor plan.** We review your show, hall, stand type, budget parameters, brand assets and objectives, then confirm the organiser's technical regulations.
2. **3D concept design.** Our in-house designers produce a 3D visualisation and walk-through so you can see and change the stand before anything is built.
3. **Engineering and approvals.** Structural drawings, load calculations, electrical layouts and rigging plans are prepared and submitted to the organiser for approval.
4. **Fabrication and graphics.** Carpentry, steel, electrical, fabric and print are produced at our workshop in Dubai Investment Park or through our partner network abroad.
5. **Install, support, dismantle.** On-site build, snagging, daily support through the show, then dismantle and storage of reusable elements for the next event.

### About: "How a Levo stand comes together" (7 steps)
Brief: Your show, your space, and what a good show looks like for you. · Concept and design: A 3D concept, refined with you until it feels right. · Engineering and approvals: Structural drawings, organiser submissions and independent sign-off, ahead of deadline. · Fabrication: Built and quality checked before it ships. · Installation: Our crew builds inside the organiser's build-up window. · Show support: A Levo team member stays on call throughout. · Dismantling: We take it down and clear the space on schedule.
